Belajar Kanji Jepang

Aplikasi belajar kanji untuk pelajar Indonesia, dari JLPT N5 sampai N1.
A kanji learning app for Indonesian learners of Japanese, JLPT N5 to N1.

Download di Google Play Instagram @creativijaya

Aplikasinya

Belajar Kanji Jepang mengajarkan kanji dengan urutan goresan, cara baca onyomi dan kunyomi, contoh kata, kuis, dan review berkala supaya kanji tidak cepat lupa. Gratis dengan iklan, dan ada langganan tanpa iklan.

In English: Belajar Kanji Jepang teaches kanji with stroke order, on and kun readings, example words, quizzes and spaced review. It is free with ads, with an ad-free subscription. Android now; iOS coming soon.

Video pendek di Instagram dan Facebook (segera juga di YouTube Shorts):

Short kanji videos in Indonesian, on Instagram and Facebook and soon on YouTube Shorts: "Kanji Hari Ini" (one kanji a day) and "Ana di Jepang" (a story about look-alike kanji).

Belajar Kanji Uploader internal tool

Belajar Kanji Uploader is a small tool that Creativijaya runs on its own computer to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public: the only Google account that signs in to it is Creativijaya's own.

Why it asks for access to Google data, and only for our own channel:

  • Upload videos (YouTube Data API): to upload our finished Shorts with their title, description and cover image.
  • Manage our channel (YouTube Data API): to add our videos to our series playlists, update their details, and read and reply to comments on our own videos.
  • Read our channel's statistics (YouTube Analytics API): views, watch time, likes and subscribers, for our own reports on which videos help learners most.

Its sign-in token stays on our computer. Nothing is sold or shared. Details: Privacy Policy, part C. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
