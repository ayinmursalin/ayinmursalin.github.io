B. The Belajar Kanji Jepang app

This part explains what the Belajar Kanji Jepang app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. You can study without signing in. Without signing in, your progress stays only on your device.

1. Data we collect

a. Account (only if you sign in)

  • When you sign in with Google (or Apple, on iOS), we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a user ID (a unique Firebase Authentication ID).
  • If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's relay address.
  • Your name and email are shown in the app (the side menu and Settings). We never send your email to analytics.

b. Learning progress

  • Your learning progress is stored on your device: kanji collections, per-kanji mastery, review schedule, completed lessons, XP, daily goal and streak, settings, and weekly study statistics.
  • If you sign in, it is also backed up to Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase) with your user ID and a backup device identifier, so you can restore it on another device.
  • You can also make a backup file on your own device.

c. Tokens and subscription

  • Your token balance and daily top-up date are stored with your user ID.
  • Your Tier Genin subscription status (active or not, and the chosen plan) is also stored with your user ID.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or bank details.
  • We use RevenueCat to verify subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase transaction data (not payment details) linked to your user ID.

d. Usage analytics

  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, for example the lessons, quizzes and reviews you open, and your streak, total XP and subscription status.
  • When you're signed in, this is linked to your user ID. Analytics also uses an app instance ID.
  • On iOS, Analytics can read the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking.

e. Crash reports

  • When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a technical report: device model, OS and app version, and the error trace. We don't link these reports to your account.

f. Ads (free users)

  • Users without a subscription see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address (not GPS), device information, and ad interactions.
  • On iOS, the advertising ID is used only if you allow it in the "Allow Tracking" prompt. In some regions, such as the EU or UK, we ask for your consent first. Without permission or consent, the ads you see are non-personalised.
  • Ad events (for example ad loaded and per-impression revenue) are also sent to RevenueCat with your user ID, for our revenue reports.

g. Notifications

  • Study reminders are created and scheduled on your device.
  • Push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which holds a device token. We don't store that token on our servers. Campaigns target groups of users based on analytics data.

We do not collect precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, video or microphone, health data, financial details or browsing history. Kanji search stays on your device.

2. How we use data

  • To run the app's features: the account, backup and restore, tokens, the subscription and the widgets.
  • To fix errors and improve the app through analytics.
  • To show ads so the app stays free.
  • To send reminders and notifications, if you allow them.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who processes data

We use these service providers. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Google Sign-In, AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform. Google Privacy Policy; how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Apple (iOS): Sign in with Apple and the App Store. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Data may be stored and processed on these providers' servers outside Indonesia. They apply equivalent data protection.

4. How long we keep data

  • On-device data is kept until you sign out, delete the app or delete your account.
  • The cloud backup, token balance and subscription status are kept until you delete your account.
  • Analytics data is kept per Google Analytics' retention settings. Crash reports are kept about 90 days.
  • Purchase records are kept by the App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat as the law requires.

5. Deleting your data and your rights

  • On Android: email creativijaya@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete account". Within 30 days we delete your Firebase account, the cloud backup, the token balance and the subscription status stored on our servers.
  • On iOS: go to Settings (Pengaturan) → Hapus Akun. This deletes the same data, plus the data on the device. Because it is the same account, it also deletes your progress for the Android app.
  • Signing out (Keluar) removes the data from the device but keeps the cloud backup.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.
  • Controlling ads: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the app shows ad privacy options in its own Settings, you can change your consent there.

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27/2022) you may request access to, correction, deletion and a copy of your personal data, and withdraw consent. Contact us at creativijaya@gmail.com.

6. Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you're a parent and believe your child gave us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The cloud backup can be read and changed only by the account that owns it. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

C. Belajar Kanji Uploader

Belajar Kanji Uploader is an internal tool that Creativijaya runs on its own computer to publish and manage the videos on our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public: the only Google account that signs in to it is Creativijaya's own. It uses the YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API). By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

1. What Google user data it accesses

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): it sends our own video files to our channel with their title, description, tags, cover image and privacy setting.
  • Manage our channel (youtube.force-ssl): it reads and updates the details of our own videos, adds them to our own playlists, and reads the comments on our own videos (comment text and the commenter's public channel name) so we can reply to them.
  • Channel statistics (yt-analytics.readonly): it reads our channel's reports, such as views, watch time, likes, shares and subscriber changes per video.

It does not access any other Google service, any other channel's private data, or the data of people who watch our videos beyond the public comments on our own videos.

2. How it uses that data

  • Only to provide its features to us: posting our videos, organising them into playlists, answering comments, and writing internal reports on which videos help learners most.
  • Data is shown only to us (the channel owner). It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not transferred to anyone else.
  • Belajar Kanji Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage, cookies and security

  • Its sign-in (OAuth) token is stored only on our own computer, in a private settings file that is never uploaded or shared. It runs no server and has no database.
  • It stores no cookies. Signing in happens once, on Google's own sign-in page in the browser.
  • All communication with Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Comment and video details it reads are kept at most 30 days. Channel statistics are kept in our internal reports, and the tool re-confirms its access to them at least every 30 days.

4. Sharing

We share no data from the tool with anyone. The only party it talks to is Google (YouTube), to do what is described above.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. The tool's own sign-out command also revokes the token with Google and deletes it from our computer.
  • After access is revoked, we delete the data the tool stored within 7 days. You can also ask us to delete it by email; we do so within 7 days.
  • Deleting the tool's local data does not delete videos, playlists or comments on YouTube. Those are managed in YouTube Studio.

Questions or complaints about the tool: creativijaya@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the "Effective" date above and announce important changes in the app or on this page.

B. Aplikasi Belajar Kanji Jepang

1. Data yang kami kumpulkan

c. Token dan langganan

  • Saldo token dan tanggal isi ulang harian disimpan bersama ID penggunamu.
  • Status langganan Tier Genin (aktif atau tidak, dan paket yang dipilih) juga disimpan bersama ID penggunamu.

  • Kami memakai RevenueCat untuk memeriksa langganan. RevenueCat menerima data transaksi pembelian (bukan data pembayaran) yang terhubung dengan ID penggunamu.

d. Analitik penggunaan

  • Kami memakai Google Analytics for Firebase untuk memahami cara aplikasi dipakai, misalnya pelajaran, kuis dan review yang dibuka, serta streak, total XP dan status langganan.

  • Data penayangan iklan (misalnya iklan dimuat dan pendapatan per tayangan) juga dikirim ke RevenueCat bersama ID penggunamu, untuk laporan pendapatan kami.

g. Notifikasi

  • Pengingat belajar dibuat dan dijadwalkan di perangkatmu sendiri.
  • Notifikasi push dikirim lewat Firebase Cloud Messaging, yang menyimpan token perangkat. Kami tidak menyimpan token itu di server kami. Kampanye dikirim ke kelompok pengguna berdasarkan data analitik.

Yang tidak kami kumpulkan: lokasi akurat (GPS), kontak, foto, video atau mikrofon, data kesehatan, data keuangan, dan riwayat penjelajahan. Pencarian kanji tetap di perangkatmu.

2. Untuk apa data dipakai

  • Menjalankan fitur aplikasi: akun, backup dan pemulihan, token, langganan, serta widget.
  • Memperbaiki error dan meningkatkan aplikasi lewat analitik.
  • Menampilkan iklan supaya aplikasi tetap gratis.
  • Mengirim pengingat dan informasi lewat notifikasi, jika kamu mengizinkannya.

Kami tidak menjual data pribadimu.

Kami memakai penyedia layanan berikut. Mereka memproses data sesuai kebijakan privasi masing-masing:

C. Belajar Kanji Uploader

Belajar Kanji Uploader adalah alat internal yang dijalankan Creativijaya di komputernya sendiri untuk mengunggah dan mengelola video di kanal YouTube kami sendiri. Alat ini tidak ditawarkan ke publik: satu-satunya akun Google yang masuk ke alat ini adalah akun Creativijaya sendiri. Alat ini memakai YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API dan YouTube Analytics API). Dengan memakainya, kamu setuju dengan Persyaratan Layanan YouTube. Cara Google menangani data dijelaskan di Kebijakan Privasi Google.

1. Data Google yang diakses

  • Mengunggah video (youtube.upload): mengirim file video kami ke kanal kami, bersama judul, deskripsi, tag, gambar sampul dan pengaturan privasinya.
  • Mengelola kanal kami (youtube.force-ssl): membaca dan memperbarui detail video kami sendiri, memasukkannya ke playlist kami sendiri, dan membaca komentar di video kami sendiri (isi komentar dan nama kanal publik pemberi komentar) supaya bisa kami balas.
  • Statistik kanal (yt-analytics.readonly): membaca laporan kanal kami, seperti penayangan, waktu tonton, suka, bagikan, dan perubahan jumlah subscriber per video.

Alat ini tidak mengakses layanan Google lain, data pribadi kanal lain, atau data penonton selain komentar publik di video kami sendiri.

2. Untuk apa data dipakai

  • Hanya untuk fitur alat ini bagi kami: memposting video kami, menyusunnya ke playlist, membalas komentar, dan membuat laporan internal tentang video mana yang paling membantu pelajar.
  • Data hanya ditampilkan ke kami (pemilik kanal). Data tidak dipakai untuk iklan, tidak dijual, tidak dipakai untuk melatih model AI, dan tidak dipindahkan ke pihak lain.
  • Penggunaan dan pemindahan informasi yang diterima Belajar Kanji Uploader dari Google API mematuhi Google API Services User Data Policy, termasuk persyaratan Limited Use.

3. Penyimpanan, cookie dan keamanan

  • Token masuk (OAuth) alat ini disimpan hanya di komputer kami sendiri, di file pengaturan pribadi yang tidak pernah diunggah atau dibagikan. Alat ini tidak punya server atau database.
  • Alat ini tidak menyimpan cookie. Proses masuk hanya sekali, di halaman masuk Google sendiri di browser.
  • Semua komunikasi dengan Google memakai koneksi terenkripsi (HTTPS).
  • Detail komentar dan video yang dibaca disimpan paling lama 30 hari. Statistik kanal disimpan di laporan internal kami, dan alat ini memastikan ulang aksesnya paling tidak setiap 30 hari.

4. Berbagi data

Kami tidak membagikan data dari alat ini kepada siapa pun. Satu-satunya pihak yang dihubungi alat ini adalah Google (YouTube), untuk hal-hal di atas.

5. Mencabut akses dan menghapus data

  • Akses bisa dicabut kapan saja di halaman setelan keamanan Google: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Perintah keluar (sign-out) di alat ini juga mencabut token di Google dan menghapusnya dari komputer kami.
  • Setelah akses dicabut, kami menghapus data yang disimpan alat ini dalam 7 hari. Kamu juga bisa meminta penghapusan lewat email; kami menghapusnya dalam 7 hari.
  • Menghapus data lokal alat ini tidak menghapus video, playlist atau komentar di YouTube. Semua itu dikelola di YouTube Studio.
